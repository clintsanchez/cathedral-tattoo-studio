## 1.4 How the document itself looks

The document is set in this brand's own system, not in the reference's.

- **Every page is `#0A0A0A`.** Body text is Text `#E8E4DC`. Secondary text is Text muted `#A9A29A`.
- **Section-opener plates** are `#0A0A0A` with the section number as a Label in Gold primary, the section title in Cinzel capitals in Text, and one gold hairline 96 px long beneath it. Nothing else. Leave the rest of the plate black.
- **Specimen plates** are Surface `#141414` with a 1 px border in `rgba(185, 144, 64, 0.18)`. Nested panels are Elevated `#1E1C19`.
- **Table header rows** are Elevated `#1E1C19` with Label-style text in Gold primary (5.77 to 1). Body rows are on the page ground with 1 px row rules in the border colour. No vertical rules, no zebra stripes.
- **Callouts** use a 4 px left rule on a Surface panel: Gold primary for notes, Warning `#E8A33D` for unresolved items, Error `#D9534F` for prohibitions. Each callout also carries an icon and a word ("Note", "Unresolved", "Never"), because colour is never the only signal.
- **Code** is set in the system monospace stack (`ui-monospace, Menlo, Consolas, monospace`) on Surface. This is the only place a third typeface appears, and it appears only in the kit document.
- **The running header** carries "Cathedral Tattoo Studio brand kit" in Small, Text muted, at left, and the section name at right. **The running footer** carries the proposal line and the page number. Neither carries a logo.
- **The lockup appears on the cover and on the logo pages, and nowhere else in the document chrome.**
- "Prepared by BlakSheep Creative" is set in type. No agency logo file is supplied, so none is drawn or generated; leave a labelled slot for it on the cover and the closing page.
- The PDF is a screen document. Do not make a light edition for printing, and do not invert any page.

This is **not a rebrand.** The name, the logo and gold on black stay. Do not propose a new logo, a simplified logo, a horizontal lockup, a monogram, a wordmark, a mascot, a new name or a sub-brand for any artist.

# 3. Brand foundation (agency proposal, `CONFIRM BEFORE PUBLISHING`)

## The design idea
**A cathedral mark over work that carries weight, in a room with the door open.**

Black is the field. Gold is gilding. The mark is gothic; the words are plain. The severity of the mark is offset on purpose by what sits beside it: warm directional light in the photographs, faces, the room, sentence-case type and generous space. Show fewer pieces, larger.

## The open decision, B-05: carry it, do not resolve it
In September 2026 the agency positioned the studio at the top of its local market. The live studio takes walk-ins six days a week and has no minimum. Clint Sanchez of BlakSheep Creative has not ruled on which way the brand goes. The kit is written to be true either way:

- **Working position:** "Considered work, and a room that is easy to walk into."
- **Commissioned work leads every layout.** The largest image, the first section and the first sentence are about the work.
- **Walk-ins are stated plainly as a fact, never as a headline.** They appear in the descriptor, in the hours block and in a profile attribute. They never appear as a display line, a banner, a button or the subject of a post.
- **Quality is expressed through restraint, never claimed in words:** more black, fewer and larger images, more space, named artists. The words in register item 4 of section 10.2 are never used.
- **No layout promises selectivity the studio does not practise.** Nothing says or implies that the studio turns work away, limits intake or keeps a list.

Render B-05 in the kit's section 14 as question 1, with this explanation and a list of the pieces it touches: the hero, the descriptor, the open-dates block, the Google Business Profile description and the position line in section 01.

## Core message
"Cathedral helps people carrying one piece that has to be right find an artist who treats it that way." This is a strategic anchor for the writer. It is not a headline and it is not printed on any template.

## Tagline and descriptor
- **Proposed tagline:** "Work worth carrying". `CONFIRM BEFORE PUBLISHING`: nobody at the studio has seen it.
- **Proposed descriptor, which travels with it:** "Custom tattoo work in Denham Springs. Walk in, or book ahead." `CONFIRM BEFORE PUBLISHING`.
- The tagline is a supporting line. It is never part of the logo artwork, never sits inside the logo's clear space, and is never set in blackletter. **Nothing permanent carries it until it is approved.** Build it as a toggled slot on the hero, the slide masters and the social closing card, each carrying the badge, and leave it off the two print layouts.
- No other tagline is offered. Do not write alternatives.

## Brand headline and supporting statement
- Headline that rests only on confirmed facts: "Custom tattoo work in Denham Springs".
- Supporting statement: "Black and grey, fine line, lettering and cover-ups. Walk in, or book ahead."

## Five key messages, each with its proof
No message is shown without its proof. A message whose proof slot is empty does not render.

| # | Message | Proof that may be shown |
|---|---|---|
| A | **The work is the proof.** The studio does not describe its skill. It shows the piece, large, and says who made it | Photographs from `assets/photos/`, each with its credit line |
| B | **Planned before anything is drawn.** Large work is talked through first: size, placement and how many sittings | The how-it-works block. `CONFIRM BEFORE PUBLISHING`: the steps, which nobody at the studio has confirmed |
| C | **You will know when you can get in.** Open dates are posted each month | The open-dates block. `CONFIRM BEFORE PUBLISHING`: where the dates are posted and the dates themselves |
| D | **You are listened to first.** | "He took my ideas and brought them to life even better than I imagined." Keely, Google review |

## What is deliberately not written
The studio's story, purpose, mission, vision and values belong to the people at the studio, and none of them has stated any. Do not write them. In the kit's section 01, show each as an empty labelled slot: "Not written. To come from the studio." Build no "about us" paragraph, no origin story and no list of values.

## Design principles
1. **Black is the field, not the background.** The dark grounds are about 83 per cent of any layout. Do not lighten the brand to make it feel friendlier. That is a different brand.
2. **Spend the gold.** Gold is 12 to 15 per cent of a layout at most, and usually far less. Two gold elements competing in one view means one of them is wrong.
3. **The work outranks the brand.** Every layout decision resolves in favour of showing the tattoo more clearly. Chrome shrinks, images grow. The logo appears once per view and does not follow the reader down the page.
4. **Show less, larger.** Twenty pieces at full width, not a wall of thumbnails. A crowded grid gives every piece the same weight and reads as a volume shop.
5. **The mark is gothic; the voice is not.** Let the logo be dramatic so that nothing else has to be. One ecclesiastical voice per brand: the logo. No gothic ornament anywhere else.
6. **Let it be still.** No motion for its own sake. The register is calm, and stillness is how calm reads on a screen.

## What the brand refuses to be
- **Not loud.** No aggressive imagery as branding, no neon, no flames, no drips, no distressed textures. Skulls, daggers and the like belong in the portfolio where a client asked for them, never in the brand.
- **Not a costume.** No second blackletter, no liturgical prose, no scrollwork, no drop caps, no coined gothic names for pages or services.
- **Not a religious organisation.** The name and the mark are architectural. The brand makes no statement of faith.
- **Not priced in public.** No figure, no floor, no offer.
- **Not older, larger or more decorated than it is.** No years, no counts, no awards, no rank.
- **Not defined against anybody.** It is described by what it does, never by another shop.
- **Not a place for an impulse.** Nothing pitches party tattoos, dares or a decision made on the night.

# 5. Logo rules

## 5.1 What it is
A vertical lockup, taller than it is wide: a gothic cathedral facade with spires, a rose window at its centre and a cross at its peak, above the word "Cathedral" in a blackletter face, above "Tattoo Studio" in small capitals between two dots. It is rendered in a metallic gold with a continuous gradient, on flat black. It is the studio's own artwork.

It exists only as a raster image. Analysis of the source (recorded in `assets/logos/README.md`) found a continuous-tone render with about 130,000 distinct colours and two sides that were drawn independently, so there is no vector behind it and none can be traced from it. Clint Sanchez of BlakSheep Creative decided on 2026-09-05 that the logo ships as it is, with no redraw. The files in `assets/logos/` are cut and scaled from that artwork. Nothing in them was redrawn, recoloured or reconstructed.

Three consequences for layout:

1. **The lockup is portrait.** Its file is 1200 × 1499 px, a ratio of 4 to 5. The artwork fills about 87 per cent of the file's width and 92 per cent of its height. There is no horizontal version, so a conventional wide header logo is not possible. Section 9 solves this with a centred masthead.
2. **It does not reduce.** It is legible at 128 px wide, illegible at 64 px, a smear at 32 px and a blob at 16 px. Below 128 px a different piece of the same artwork takes over (section 5.5).
3. **It needs black around it.** It has no version for a light ground and none may be made.

## 5.4 Clear space and minimum sizes

- **Clear space:** X on every side, where X is the height of the cross finial at the top of the mark, about 8 per cent of the lockup's height. Measure X outward from the edge of the file. Nothing enters it: no type, no image edge, no button, no hairline.
- **Minimum, full lockup:** 128 px wide on screen, which is 160 px tall. This floor is measured, not estimated.
- **Below 128 px wide:** do not scale the lockup down. Step to the rose window or the cross (section 5.5).
- **No print minimum has been measured.** The artwork is not available for print (section 5.8).

| 160 px | 200 px | 16 px |
| 200 px | 250 px | 20 px |

Placement sizes: website masthead 160 px wide on desktop and 128 px on mobile, centred; a hero or cover placement 240 px or wider; link preview, the supplied `og-card-1200x630.png`, whole. In a 1080 px social graphic the lockup must be at least 360 px wide to stay legible in a feed, so it appears only on opening and closing cards. Never scale any file above its own pixel size.

**Once per view.** The lockup appears once on a page, at the top, and does not repeat lower down. It never rides in a sticky bar. A carousel carries it on one card, not on every card.

## 5.7 Never
Redraw, trace, vectorise or regenerate it. Re-letter the name in any font and call it the logo. Recolour it, flatten it to one colour, make it white, make it black, or tint it. Outline, bevel, shadow, glow or emboss it. Stretch, squash, rotate, tilt, mirror or crop it. Separate the building from the wordmark, or cut any piece out other than the supplied rose window and cross. Scale the lockup below 128 px wide. Scale any file above its pixel size. Place it on a light ground, on gold, on a gradient, on a pattern or over busy imagery. Place it over a tattoo, a face or text. Add a tagline, an address, a year or a handle to the artwork. Repeat it down a page. Put it in a sticky bar. Use the RC monogram or the CTS badge in its place. Use any other "logo" found in the photographs or online.

## 6.3 Usage ratio
Measured as a share of a full scrolled page or a whole canvas, photographs excluded: Ground about 70 per cent · Surface and Elevated together about 13 per cent, so the dark field is about 83 per cent · gold of all three kinds 12 per cent at most on a page and 15 per cent at most on a single graphic, and usually far less · off-white text about 5 per cent. Render this as a ratio bar. The check: with eyes half shut, the layout reads as black with a little gilding. A layout that reads as gold has failed.

## 6.4 Rules
- **Dark by default and dark throughout.** No light section, no white card, no pale panel, no inverted footer.
- **Gold is spent, not spread.** In any one view: one gold button at most, one hairline at most, and gold text limited to links and one eyebrow. No gold backgrounds, gold panels, gold bands or gold photo frames.
- **The primary button** is solid Gold primary with a Ground label. **The secondary button** is a 1 px Gold primary outline with a Gold primary label on a transparent fill.
- **Body text is Text, never gold.** Gold text is for links, eyebrow labels and the hairline's companion line. A paragraph is never gold.
- **Pure white `#FFFFFF` and pure black `#000000` are not tokens.** Text is `#E8E4DC`. The ground is `#0A0A0A`. The one exception is the `#000000` panel that the opaque lockup files may need (section 5.2).
- **No second accent hue.** No red, no purple, no blue, no green in any brand material. The status colours are not accents.
- **No gradients, glows, drop shadows, metallic effects, foil effects or textures made in the layout.** The only gradient in the brand is the one inside the logo artwork. Do not imitate it on a button, a rule or a heading.
- **Photographs keep their own colour.** No gold tint, no duotone, no black and white conversion of a colour piece, no warm grade.
- **Colour is never the only signal:** links are underlined, the selected filter has a tick as well as a border, the current navigation item has an underline, a badge has an icon and words.
- **The single star in the rating line** is Gold primary. No other star is drawn anywhere.
- **Print.** No print colour reference exists. Publish no CMYK or Pantone value as a specification. Mark both print layouts "Artwork not print-ready".
- Do not introduce any hue outside this list.

# 7. Typography (agency proposal, `CONFIRM BEFORE PUBLISHING`)

**Two families, each with one job.**

- **Cinzel**, for H1 and H2 only (and the Display size, which is an H1 in the hero). It is drawn from Roman inscriptional capitals, the lettering carved into stone buildings, so it carries the architectural weight of the mark without imitating the blackletter. Weight 600. Set in capitals, letterspaced, in short lines.
- **Source Sans 3**, for H3, body, interface text, captions, labels, buttons, numerals and every quotation. Weights 400 and 600. A humanist sans that stays legible as light text on a dark ground.

Both are free on Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License. Fallback stacks, from the token file: `"Cinzel", Georgia, serif` and `"Source Sans 3", system-ui, sans-serif`.

**The blackletter in the logo is unidentified and exists only inside the logo image.** It is never set as live type, so there is no token for it. Do not look for a match, and do not use any blackletter, Old English, uncial or other gothic face anywhere. A second gothic voice would turn the brand into a costume.

**Never:** script or tattoo-script faces · distressed or grunge faces · slab serifs · geometric sans faces · handwritten faces · any third family in brand material · italics · weights above 600 · text on a curve · outlined, shadowed or gradient-filled type.

## 7.2 Rules
- **Sentence case is the rule for the words.** Type every heading in sentence case in the source and let the style set Cinzel in capitals. The text then stays correct if the face changes. Never type a heading in capitals by hand, and never use Title Case.
- **Cinzel lines are short.** 28 characters a line at most, three lines at most, about eight words at most. If a heading needs more, it is an H3 in Source Sans 3.
- **Cinzel is never used** for body text, buttons, navigation, captions, credit lines, artist names, review quotes, phone numbers, addresses, hours, dates or anything below 26 px.
- **Numerals are always Source Sans 3.** The phone number, the hours, the address, the rating and every date are set in Source Sans 3, with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` wherever figures are stacked.
- **Headings and body text are Text `#E8E4DC`.** An eyebrow Label may be Gold primary. A heading is never gold.
- **Measure:** 60 to 75 characters a line for body text; text columns no wider than 720 px. Left aligned. Centre only the masthead, a hero line and a standalone call to action. No justified text.
- **Emphasis is weight 600.** No italics, no underlining except links, no capitals for emphasis in body text.
- **Dark grounds need room.** Body line height is 1.6 and is never tightened. Light text on black looks heavier than it is, so do not add weight to compensate.
- No exclamation mark and no ellipsis in the brand's own words. No emoji. No dashes used as punctuation. No hashtags inside a sentence.
- Body text is 18 px on screen. Nothing on screen is below 14 px.

## 7.3 Social and slide scales
Do not shrink web sizes into a graphic. Derive each from the two families:

- **Social, on a 1080 × 1350 canvas:** headline in Cinzel 600 capitals at 72 to 88 px, line height 1.15, six words at most · support line in Source Sans 3 400 at 44 px, line height 1.4, one sentence · label in Source Sans 3 600 capitals at 30 px, 0.08em · review quote in Source Sans 3 400 at 52 to 60 px, 40 words at most · credit line in Source Sans 3 400 at 36 px · smallest text allowed 36 px · margins 80 px on all sides.
- **Slides, 1920 × 1080:** body never below 28 px, 30 words a slide at most, headline in Cinzel 600 capitals at 72 px or larger.
- **Print layouts:** smallest text 8 pt; 10 pt for the aftercare steps.

Provide the scales as specimen cards on Ground, with one real sentence from section 12 set at each level.

# 8. Layout, shape and motion

- **Spacing scale, and nothing between:** 8 · 16 · 32 · 64 · 96 · 128 px (`--cts-space-1` to `--cts-space-6`). Inside a component: 8 between a label and its value, 16 between related items, 32 between groups.
- **Container** 1200 px (`--cts-container`). **Text measure** 720 px.
- **Grid:** 4 columns under 640 px (16 px gutter, 16 px side margin); 8 columns between 640 and 1023 px (16 px gutter, 32 px margin); 12 columns at 1024 px and wider (32 px gutter, 32 px margin).
- **Section padding, top and bottom:** 64 px on mobile and tablet; 96 px on desktop; 128 px for the hero and the closing block only. When a layout feels slightly too empty, it is probably right. Do not fill the space.
- **Radius:** 6 px for buttons, filter chips and badges (`--cts-radius-sm`); 12 px for cards (`--cts-radius-md`); 20 px for the rare large panel (`--cts-radius-lg`). **Photographs standing alone are square-cornered.** A photograph inside a card is clipped only by the card's own corners. No pill shapes, no circles except the avatar crop.
- **Borders:** 1 px in the Border colour for cards and dividers; 1 px Gold primary on the secondary button; 1 px Gold deep where a boundary must be seen.
- **Shadows:** none. The system has no shadow tokens. A shadow is invisible on black and a glow is forbidden. Cards are separated by ground colour and a 1 px edge.
- **The gold hairline** is the one decorative device (section 11). One per view.
- **Section rhythm:** Ground, then Surface, alternating; never two Surface sections in a row. Elevated is for panels inside a section, never for a whole section. Each section opens the same way: optional eyebrow (Label, Gold primary), H2, optional Lead, then one kind of content. A photograph at least every second section.
- **Dividers:** sections are separated by the change of ground and by space, not by a line. No arches, pointed frames, tracery, torn edges, angled cuts or ornamental rules.
- **Image first.** In any section that holds a photograph of a piece, the photograph is the largest element, and the text sits beside it or beneath it, never on it.
- **Tap targets:** 44 × 44 px at minimum, 8 px between neighbours. Buttons are 48 px tall.
- **Motion:** transitions of 200 ms or shorter with gentle easing, on hover and focus only: a change of colour or opacity, never a scale, a lift, a slide or a bounce. Nothing moves by itself. No autoplay, parallax, marquee, countdown, animated gradient, shimmer on the gold or self-advancing carousel. All transitions are removed under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Dark theme only.** No light mode and no theme switch.
- **No pop-ups, chat bubbles, cookie-style promotional bars or sticky offers.** The only fixed elements are the compact bar on desktop and the mobile action bar.

## 11.3 Rules

# 14. Accessibility (binding)

A page or graphic that fails one of these is not finished. A dark system is easy to make beautiful and unreadable; these lines exist to prevent that.

1. Text meets WCAG 2.1 AA: 4.5 to 1, or 3 to 1 at 24 px and larger (19 px and larger if bold). Body text in Text meets AAA on all three grounds. Use only the pairs in section 6.2.
2. Anything that carries meaning without text (icons, focus rings, the selected filter's edge, the star in the rating line, a boundary that marks a control) meets 3 to 1 against its ground.
3. Gold deep never carries text below 24 px and never sits on Elevated. The Border colour never carries meaning by itself.
4. The primary button's label is Ground on Gold primary, 6.72 to 1. No off-white or white label ever sits on gold.
5. Nothing is ever placed on a light ground, so no gold-on-white pair can occur.
6. Colour is never the only signal: links are underlined; the selected filter has a tick; the current navigation item has an underline; a badge has an icon and words; a status has text.
7. Body text is 18 px on screen; nothing is below 14 px. Text reflows at 320 px wide and at 200 per cent zoom with no sideways scrolling of the page and no clipped text. No text is set in an image on the website.
8. Light text on black is tiring in long runs: body line height stays at 1.6, columns stay at 720 px or narrower, and no paragraph is set in Cinzel or in capitals.
9. Every control is at least 44 × 44 px with 8 px between neighbours.
10. Every interactive element has a visible focus state: a 3 px Gold bright outline with a 2 px offset. Focus is never removed.
11. Everything works with the keyboard, in reading order. The first link on every page is "Skip to content". The filter row and the mobile menu are operable from the keyboard.
12. No autoplay, no self-advancing carousel. Transitions are 200 ms or shorter and are removed under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
13. The mobile action bar never covers the last line of the page.
14. Every meaningful image has alt text that says what is shown and who made it. The hairline has empty alt text. The logo link's accessible name is "Cathedral Tattoo Studio, home". The cross in the compact bar has the same accessible name.
15. One H1 per page; headings descend in order. Cinzel capitals are produced by the style, not typed, so that a screen reader does not spell the words out.
16. The phone number is a real `tel:` link and is written out as text. The address and the hours are text, never an image.
17. The embedded map is outside this system's control. Wherever it appears, the address appears beside it as text.
18. Social: the smallest text in a 1080 px graphic is 36 px; body text contrast is 7 to 1 or better; alt text on every post. Print layouts: 8 pt at minimum, 10 pt for the aftercare steps.
