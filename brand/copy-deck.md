# Copy Deck v1 — Cathedral Tattoo Studio

*2026-09-05. Written in the register set by `voice.md`, against the positioning in
`positioning.md`, and scrubbed line by line against all eight guardrails.*

> **⚠️ Draft for BSC review, then Roger's approval.** Nothing here ships until R-18
> and R-19 clear. **Roger must read the first-person lines aloud** — if he wouldn't say
> it, it's wrong, no matter how well it's written.

**House rules applied throughout:** no em dashes · sentence case · contractions ·
active voice · second person singular · no exclamation marks · paragraphs under four
lines · numerals for prices, dates and sessions.

**Premium is expressed, never claimed.** There isn't a single premium adjective below.
The position is carried by restraint, specificity and what the copy declines to say.

## 3. Website

**Hero**

> # Work worth carrying
>
> Custom tattoo work in Denham Springs. Black and grey, fine line, lettering and
> cover-ups, booked by appointment and planned properly.
>
> [ See the work ]   [ Check open dates ]

**Intro, below hero**

> Some tattoos are decoration. Some you carry for the rest of your life.
>
> Most of what comes through here is the second kind: a portrait of someone who died, a
> verse that matters, a piece that finishes a sleeve you started years ago. That work
> takes planning, and it takes more than one sitting.
>
> So we plan it. You'll know the size, the placement, how many sessions it needs and
> what it costs before anything permanent happens.

**How it works** *(the section no local competitor has)*

> **1. Tell us what you're thinking about.**
> Send a reference photo, roughly where you want it, and rough size. A screenshot is
> fine. You don't need to have it worked out.
>
> **2. We'll talk it through.**
> What will work on skin and what won't, how it'll age, where it should sit. If it's a
> portrait, bring the clearest photo you have. Face-on is best, but most things can be
> worked with.
>
> **3. You'll get a plan.**
> Size, placement, number of sittings, and the cost, before anything is drawn.
>
> **4. Then we tattoo it.**
> Large pieces run across several sittings, spaced a few weeks apart to heal.

**Portfolio section intro**

> Sorted by what the piece is, not by style. Every piece is credited to the artist who
> made it.

**Availability**

> Open dates are posted at the start of each month. First come, first served.
>
> [ This month's dates ]

⚠️ No countdowns, no "spots going fast", no scarcity language beyond the plain fact.

## 6. Review request

Goal 2 is review volume and there are currently zero. Send this by text a few days
after the sitting, once it's healing well. Plain, no incentive, no pressure. **Never
offer anything in exchange for a review.**

> Hey [name], hope it's healing well. Keep it out of the sun and keep it moisturised
> this week.
>
> If you're happy with it, a quick Google review would help us out a lot. We're new, so
> the first few make a real difference. No worries if not.
>
> [link]

⚠️ "We're new" is honest, disarming, and permitted. It's a fact about the business, not
a tenure claim.

## 7. Aftercare card

Instructional voice, no personality. Every client leaves with one.

> ## Looking after it
>
> 1. Leave the wrap on for 2 hours.
> 2. Wash gently with clean hands and unscented soap. Pat dry, don't rub.
> 3. Thin layer of unscented moisturiser, 2 to 3 times a day.
> 4. No soaking for 2 weeks. No baths, pools or swimming. Showers are fine.
> 5. Keep it out of the sun. Once healed, sunscreen keeps it sharp.
> 6. It'll peel and itch. Don't pick it, don't scratch it.
>
> Anything looks wrong, call us: (225) 301-9527
>
> Cathedral Tattoo Studio · 1318 Florida Ave SW, Denham Springs

⚠️ Aftercare is health-adjacent. Have Roger confirm this matches what he tells people
in the chair before it prints.
