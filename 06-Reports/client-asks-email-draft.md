## Body

Roger,

I've been putting together the Cathedral file so we can start on the brand and the
website. Most of what I have I pulled off your Google listing and the Facebook
page, which gets me a long way, but there are a handful of things only you can
answer. A few of them are holding up work, so I've put those first.

**1. Logo files.** This is the big one. Everything I have is a screenshot off
Facebook. That's fine for looking at, but I can't build a favicon, a print piece,
or a clean header out of it — the gold has a gradient and it goes to mush when it's
resized. If whoever drew the Cathedral mark sent you an .ai, .eps, or .svg file,
that's what I need. Even the original layered file works. If you don't have one,
tell me who made it and I'll chase it down.

**2. The address.** Your August 27th post says you're moving somewhere bigger. Your
Google listing and Facebook both still show 1318 Florida Ave SW, and the LLC
paperwork lists a Bluebonnet Boulevard address in Baton Rouge. I don't want to
guess. Which one is the studio, and when does the move happen? Once an address goes
onto Google, the website, and the directory listings, changing it later is a mess —
so I'd rather sit on it a week than publish the wrong one.

**3. Hours.** Google only shows Friday, 10 to 7. What are the real hours once
you're in the new space?

**4. A paperwork question.** The state filing for CATHEDRAL TATTOO STUDIO LLC lists
Mikayla Paul as the only member. That may be exactly how you set it up, and it
doesn't change anything about how we work together — but it means I can't put
"owner" next to your name on the website until you tell me how you want it
described. Until then I'll write you as "tattoo artist at Cathedral Tattoo Studio,"
which is accurate either way. Just let me know what's right.

**5. Licenses.** I need the shop's body-art facility permit number and the operator
registration number for you and anyone else who tattoos there. Louisiana doesn't
publish these anywhere I can look them up, so it has to come from you. Until I have
them I'm not going to put anything on the site about licensing or sterilization —
those are the exact claims that get a shop in trouble if the paperwork doesn't
match.

**6. How you want bookings to come in.** Form on the website, phone, Instagram DM,
something else? And the best email address for you — I don't have one.

**7. Services and pricing.** What do you want to be known for, and do you have a
shop minimum? Facebook has you listed as a tattoo and piercing shop — is piercing
actually running yet, or is that for the new place?

**8. Walk-ins.** Your post says the new location will allow them. I'm not putting
"walk-ins welcome" anywhere until you tell me it's live, because that's the kind of
thing that gets someone driving over for nothing.

**9. Artists.** You mentioned you're hiring artists and piercers. Send me names as
they come on so I can build them into the site instead of bolting them on later.

**10. Older photos.** I've got a batch of your older work, but the files have your
previous shop's watermark burned into them. I cleaned up most of them and they look
good, but eight didn't come out well enough to publish. If you still have the
originals on your phone or a hard drive, those would be better than anything I can
reconstruct.

**11. Instagram and TikTok.** Send me the handles so I can link them and match the
branding.

**12. The domain.** `cathedraltattoostudio.com` was still available when I checked.
Say the word and I'll register it before someone else does.

Nothing here is urgent except the logo files and the address — those two are what
I'm actually waiting on. The rest you can send as you get to it.

Clint
