## Subject

Two quick things, and can I get 15 minutes on the phone

## Body

Roger,

I've been building out the Cathedral file, and it's in good shape. The logo's set up
for the website and socials, and I've got the shop's Google listing ready to fill in
properly. A few things I need from you before I can put any of it live.

**Two quick ones first, because they're time-sensitive.**

**1. cathedraltattoostudio.com is still available.** I checked again yesterday. It's
about $15 a year and it's the exact name of the shop. Someone else grabbing it would be
a genuine pain to work around, and it happens. Say the word and I'll register it in
your name today.

**2. Is the Instagram @cathedraltattoostudio yours?** There's an account on that handle
with no posts and 6 followers. If you or someone at the shop set it up, I need the
login. If it isn't yours, someone else is sitting on the obvious handle and we should
pick a different one before the website goes up. Also, what's the TikTok, if there is
one?

**Can I get 15 minutes on the phone?**

This is the one I can't work around. I can pull your work, your hours and your
paperwork off the internet, but I can't get the things that make the site sound like
you instead of like every other shop. Why you opened Cathedral, why that name, what you
want the new place to feel like when somebody walks in, and where you want this to be
in a few years. One chair or a room full of artists changes what I build now, and it's
cheaper to know today.

While I've got you I'd also ask about your last twenty or so bookings. What people came
in for, why, and how they found you. That would replace a lot of educated guessing on
my end with actual facts.

Pick a time that works and I'll call you.

**Things I need in writing, when you get a chance.**

**3. Your licences.** The shop's body art facility permit number, and the operator
registration number for you and anyone else who tattoos there. Louisiana doesn't
publish these anywhere I can look up, so it has to come from you. Until I have them I'm
not putting anything on the site about licensing or sterilisation. Those are exactly
the claims that cause trouble if the paperwork doesn't match.

**4. A paperwork question.** The state filing for Cathedral Tattoo Studio LLC lists
Mikayla Paul as the only member. That may be exactly how you set it up, and it doesn't
change anything about how we work together. It just means I can't put "owner" next to
your name until you tell me how you want it described. For now I've written you as
"tattoo artist at Cathedral Tattoo Studio," which is accurate either way. Tell me what's
right and I'll change it.

**5. Who made the logo, and with what?** I'm asking because if it was made with an AI
tool, it may not be something you can legally protect, which matters if another shop
ever copies it. Not urgent, and not a reason to change it. I just want to know what you
have.

**Quick answers when you have a minute.**

6. **Hours.** Google only shows Friday, 10 to 7. What are the real hours?
7. **Best email address for you**, and how you want enquiries to come in. Website form,
   phone, text, Instagram DM?
8. **What do you actually offer**, and is piercing running yet? Facebook lists the shop
   as tattoo and piercing, and I don't want to advertise something that isn't live.
9. **Walk-ins.** Are you taking them now, or is that for the new place? I won't put
   "walk-ins welcome" anywhere until you tell me, because that's the kind of thing that
   has somebody driving over for nothing.
10. **Artists.** Send me names as people come on, so I can build them in properly
    instead of bolting them on later.
11. **The older photos.** I've got a batch of your earlier work, but the files have your
    previous shop's watermark on them. I cleaned up most of them and they look good, but
    eight didn't come out well enough to publish. If you've still got the originals on
    your phone or a drive, those would be better than anything I can patch.

**One thing I'd push you on.**

You mentioned there's no shop minimum. I'd think about setting one, and taking a deposit
when someone books.

Not to make the shop look expensive. It's about protecting your chair time. Without a
minimum, your calendar fills up with small quick pieces, and those are the hours you
need free for the big work that actually builds your portfolio and brings people in.
Shops around here run about $100 as a minimum. Given the kind of work you're doing, I'd
put yours a bit above that.

The deposit matters more than the number. It comes off the price of the tattoo, it holds
the date, and it more or less ends no-shows, which is the thing that quietly costs you
the most.

Your call entirely. You know your costs and your customers better than I do. But it's
the single easiest change I'd make to the way the shop books work.

Nothing here is urgent except the domain and the Instagram handle. The rest you can send
over as you get to it, and the phone call whenever suits you.

Clint
