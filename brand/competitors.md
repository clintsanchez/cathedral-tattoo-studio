# Competitor Brand Analysis — Cathedral Tattoo Studio

*Researched 2026-09-05 from live public sources. Tier ③ unless marked otherwise.
Nothing here came from the client. Review counts and ratings are as reported by the
directories named and were not independently re-counted.*

## 01 — Competitive landscape

Denham Springs is not an underserved market — it is a saturated one, and the
saturation is literal: **Cathedral (1318 Florida Ave SW), Safe Voyage (1500 Florida
Ave SW) and Tattootopia (2320 Florida Ave SW) sit on the same street inside one
mile.** Add Faithful (105 N Range Ave), Burning Lotus (30891 Hwy 16), Cipher, and
the Baton Rouge shops fifteen minutes west, and a customer has a dozen options
before leaving the parish.

The category's brand monotony is severe and consistent. Nearly every shop makes the
same three claims — *clean/sterile*, *experienced*, *custom work* — in the same
order, over dark websites with white sans-serif type and a grid of tattoo photos.
These are hygiene claims in both senses: table stakes that a customer assumes and
that therefore differentiate nobody. The category assumes its audience is choosing
on trust-plus-portfolio alone, and brands accordingly: defensively, and identically.

**The exploitable weakness is not positioning. It is execution.** Two of the five
named competitors have a broken or absent web presence (verified below). The
category is competing on Facebook and word of mouth, and the shop that shows up
properly online will not have to out-position anyone to be found.

## 02 — Competitor profiles

### Tattootopia — the benchmark
*tattootopia.org · 2320 Florida Ave SW Ste B · one mile up the same street*
⚠️ **Roger's prior shop, and a BSC pro-bono client.** Handle with care; see §07.

- **Positioning:** the parish's quality leader. "Livingston Parish's Premier Tattoo
  Studio"; "The utopia of all tattooing — the premier tattoo studio in the heart of
  Denham Springs, Louisiana."
- **Claims:** "Award-winning artists with 20+ years of experience"; "clean, safe, and
  sterile environment"; appointment-based with possible same-day.
- **Voice:** professional but unstuffy. "Call or text today to get booked!"
- **Visual:** black ground, white logo and type, modern and clean. Restrained.
- **Artists listed:** Jeff Thibodaux, Josh Lockhart, Nahalo Minier.
  **⚠️ Roger is not on their current artist page.** Some third-party summaries still
  describe a roster including "Roger" — likely stale cache. Worth knowing before any
  conversation about how the prior shop is handled.
- **Strength:** the only competitor with a genuinely complete, working, multi-page
  site — about, services, artists, contact, aftercare. They have done the work.
- **Weakness:** "premier" and "utopia" are unfalsifiable superlatives that every
  category leader claims. The brand asserts quality rather than demonstrating a point
  of view. Nothing about it is memorable once you close the tab.
- **Territory owned:** *established authority.* Longevity and credentials.

### Faithful Tattoo Studio — the closest brand-adjacent threat
*faithfultattoostudio.com · 105 N Range Ave Ste B · (225) 523-4474*

- **Positioning:** "a locally owned family tattoo studio." Family, faith, community.
- **Proof:** ~94% recommended across ~23 Facebook reviews; reviewers name artists
  Travis and Courtney and repeatedly praise collaboration and cleanliness. Several
  note prices are not the lowest and say the quality justifies it — that shop has
  successfully sold value over price.
- **Services:** tattoos and piercings; piercing has its own service page.
- **⚠️ Their site would not load during this research** — `faithfultattoostudio.com`
  refused HTTPS connections (ECONNREFUSED, Network Solutions IP). It may be
  http-only or broken. Either way it fails for any modern browser and for Google.
- **Strength:** real review volume, real named artists, warm family framing.
- **Weakness:** a broken site under a domain they own is worse than no site.
- **Territory owned:** *faith and family.*

> 🚨 **The finding that most affects Cathedral.** A shop one mile away already
> occupies faith-adjacent naming — and does it warmly, domestically, with
> "family" and "locally owned." Cathedral cannot position as "the Christian tattoo
> shop"; that seat is taken and Cathedral has zero reviews with which to contest it.
> Cathedral's ecclesiastical register has to mean something different from Faithful's
> — see §06.

### Burning Lotus Tattoos
*30891 Hwy 16 · (225) 271-8230 · est. 2011*

- **Positioning:** quality and range. All styles — colour, black and grey, portraits,
  custom.
- **Proof:** the strongest social proof in the set — **4.7 stars across ~168
  reviews** per Chamber of Commerce listing; 15 reviews and 48 photos on Yelp.
- **⚠️ `burninglotustattooshop.com` does not resolve** (DNS failure). The domain is
  listed across directories and is dead.
- **Strength:** review volume and fifteen years of local trust. In map-pack terms
  this is the hardest competitor to outrank.
- **Weakness:** no working website; "all styles" is a positioning vacuum.
- **Territory owned:** *the trusted local default.*

### Safe Voyage Tattoo
*safevoyagetattoo.com · 1500 Florida Ave SW · (225) 664-4005 · est. 2012 · Mon–Sat 12–7*

- **Positioning:** classic, accessible, walk-in friendly. Custom, cover-ups, flash,
  piercings. "Classic tattooing in a professional and sterile environment."
- **Strength:** the only one publishing full six-day hours, and it takes walk-ins —
  it captures the convenience customer outright. Nautical name is distinctive.
- **Weakness:** thin, dated site (a live post from 2012); "experienced artist"
  singular suggests a small operation.
- **Territory owned:** *convenience.* Open when you want, walk in.
- **Note:** 200 yards from Cathedral's listed address.

### Cipher Tattoo
Named in local listings; almost no discoverable brand presence. Not a brand threat.

### The rest
Seventh Sword Tattoo Emporium, Black Mamba, Black Torch, Art Addiction, High
Resolution, Deja Vu Piercing. Long tail — mostly Facebook-only, generic category
names.

## 03 — Competitive brand map

**Axis 1 — Convenience ← → Considered commission**
**Axis 2 — Generic category presentation ← → Distinct point of view**

- **Safe Voyage** — convenience / generic. Walk in, get tattooed.
- **Burning Lotus** — convenience-leaning / generic. Wins on trust, not on brand.
- **Tattootopia** — considered / mildly distinct. Appointment-led, asserts premium,
  but the assertion is category-standard.
- **Faithful** — considered / distinct. Family and faith is a real point of view.
- **Cathedral** — belongs at **considered / most distinct.** The mark alone is
  further from category norm than anything else in the parish.

**Open territory:** the far corner — *considered commission work with a genuine point
of view, executed properly online.* Faithful holds the nearest ground but its site is
broken and its register is domestic rather than reverent. Nobody in Denham Springs is
combining a distinctive brand with competent digital execution.

⚠️ Cathedral can occupy that corner on brand, but **cannot yet compete on proof.**
Zero reviews against Burning Lotus's 168 is the real gap, and no amount of
positioning closes it. Review generation is the first growth priority once the
address settles.

## 04 — Messaging analysis

**What the category keeps saying** — all table stakes, all worthless as differentiators:
1. *Clean / safe / sterile.* Tattootopia and Safe Voyage both lead with it.
2. *Experienced / award-winning / 20+ years.* ⚠️ Cathedral **cannot** play here at
   all — the LLC is four months old. Do not attempt an experience claim.
3. *Custom work, all styles.* Universal, and self-cancelling.
4. *Premier / best in the parish.* Unfalsifiable superlative.

**Clichés to avoid outright:** "premier", "utopia"-style superlatives, "all styles",
"we bring your vision to life", "ink" as a noun, "no regrets", any variation on
"your body is a canvas."

**What nobody is saying**
1. **Anything about the work itself.** Not one competitor explains *how* a piece gets
   made — the consult, the drawing, the sitting, the healing. The category shows
   finished photos and says "custom." A shop that shows its process would be alone.
2. **Anything about weight.** Memorial and religious pieces are the highest-stakes
   commissions a person makes, and every shop lists them as one style among many.
   Nobody addresses what it means to carry one.
3. **Anything about scarcity of time.** Roger's own posts already do this
   instinctively — month-by-month availability calendars, "now booking" ②. That is
   better marketing than anything on the competitors' sites, and it is already
   Cathedral's habit.

## 05 — Visual identity patterns

- **Colour:** black grounds, white type, occasional red. Universal. **Cathedral's
  black-and-gold is the single most differentiated colour choice in the parish** —
  gold reads as ecclesiastical and expensive where the category reads as monochrome
  and hard.
- **Typography:** clean modern sans, sometimes a tattoo-script accent. Blackletter is
  unclaimed locally.
- **Imagery:** grids of finished tattoo photos on black, shot on phones under shop
  lighting. No environmental photography, no process shots, no portraits of artists
  working. Cathedral already holds an owner-at-work portrait — the category has
  nothing like it.
- **Differentiation opportunity:** architecture. Every competitor's imagery is skin
  in close-up. Cathedral's mark promises a *place* — arches, stone, light, rose
  window. Photographing the new space as an interior, not just as a backdrop for
  arms, would be genuinely unlike anything local.

## 06 — Differentiation opportunities

**1. Be the only shop that works properly online.**
*Available because:* Burning Lotus's domain is dead, Faithful's refuses connections,
Safe Voyage's is a decade stale. Only Tattootopia has a working site.
*How to take it:* a fast, complete, correct site — services, artists, real hours,
aftercare, working booking. This is execution, not positioning, and it is the
cheapest advantage on this list.

**2. Own reverence, not religion.**
*Available because:* Faithful owns *family and faith* in a warm, domestic register.
Cathedral's mark is not domestic — it is architectural, still, weighty.
*How to take it:* speak to the seriousness of the commission, not the faith of the
customer. A cathedral is where things of weight happen; that framing serves a
memorial portrait for an atheist just as well as a crown of thorns. **This also keeps
Cathedral out of a head-on fight it would lose on review count.**

**3. Show the making.**
*Available because:* nobody local shows process.
*How to take it:* consult → drawing → sitting → healed. Roger's portfolio has healed
and multi-session work in it already. Costs nothing but a camera.

**4. Make availability the mechanic.**
*Available because:* competitors publish either "walk in" or nothing.
*How to take it:* keep doing what Roger already does — publish the open dates ②.
Turn the calendar into the site's conversion surface. It is honest scarcity: it is
simply true that there are only so many days.

**5. Name the artist, credit the work.**
*Available because:* only Tattootopia and Faithful name artists at all, and only as
names with a job title.
*How to take it:* real bios, real specialisms, work attributed per artist. Supports
the hiring push ② and matches the architecture in `architecture.md`.

**6. Own the interior.**
*Available because:* the relocation gives Cathedral a brand-new space and every
competitor's photography is skin-only.
*How to take it:* photograph the new studio properly on day one. ⚠️ Gated on the move.

## 07 — Strategic implications

**Positioning recommendation.** Considered commission work, ecclesiastical in
register, executed with a competence nobody local matches online. Not the cheapest,
not the fastest, not the oldest — the one you go to when the piece matters.
⚠️ Proposed (④). Formalized in `brand/positioning.md`; needs Roger's confirmation.

**Messaging direction.** Lead with the weight of the work and the making of it. Lead
with availability. Never lead with sterility, experience, or "custom" — those are the
three things every competitor already says, and two of them Cathedral is not
permitted to claim.

**Visual direction.** Push the gold hard. Push architecture and interior. Stay off
the category's monochrome-skin-grid default.

**What to avoid**
- Any experience, tenure or award claim. Four months old.
- Any sterility or licensing claim until the permit numbers arrive in writing.
- "Premier", "utopia", or any superlative — it would read as a direct answer to
  Tattootopia, which is exactly the fight not to pick.
- Positioning as the *Christian* tattoo shop. Faithful holds that ground with 23
  reviews and a family story; Cathedral has zero reviews.
- **Any public comparison to, or mention of, Tattootopia.** It is Roger's prior shop,
  one mile away, and a BSC pro-bono client. ⚠️ This is an unresolved BSC conflict —
  BSC now advises two shops on the same street. Flag to Clint before any competitive
  copy ships. Cathedral's copy should never reference it, and Cathedral's story
  should stand on its own without a prior-shop chapter.
