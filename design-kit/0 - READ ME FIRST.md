## What this package is

The input for two Claude Design jobs: the master brand design kit, then the social template system. A person runs both by hand at claude.ai/design. Nothing in this folder has been generated by Claude Design yet; `exports/` is empty.

The target for structure, depth and format is `assets/references/reference-kit-format_REFERENCE-ONLY.pdf`, a finished kit for a different client. It is a format reference only. None of its brand, facts, colours, type or wording applies here. It is not in version control and is uploaded by hand.

## The nature of the job

This is the definition of a brand that is five months old and has never been written down. It is not a rebrand.

**What stays:** the name; the logo exactly as it is; gold on black; the plain way the studio already writes.

**What is proposed around them:** a small set of colour roles taken from the logo's own gold; two typefaces; a position and a tagline; a voice; a rule for crediting each artist by name; and a set of templates.

**What is not proposed:** a new or redrawn logo, a vector of the logo, a light-background version, a second gothic typeface, a mascot, a sub-brand, or any colour outside `tokens/css-tokens.css`.

The idea to design from: a cathedral mark over work that carries weight, in a room with the door open. The mark is gothic and the words are plain. Black is the field. Gold is gilding, used sparingly. The severity of the mark is offset on purpose by what sits beside it: warm directional light, faces, space, and sentence-case type. Fewer pieces are shown, larger.

## How to use this kit

Before creating any page, post, card, document or image for Cathedral Tattoo Studio:

1. Read `2 - Brand Guidelines.md`.
2. Read `3 - Visual Identity.md`.
3. Read `4 - Brand Voice.md`.
4. Read `5 - Messaging.md`.
5. Read `6 - Brand Context and Prices.md` whenever an artist, a price, a place, booking, a review or any claim is involved. Its three claims lists override everything else in the kit.
6. Use only the assets in `assets/`, as listed in file 8.
7. Never redraw, trace, vectorise, re-letter, recolour or AI-generate the logo, the rose window, the cross or the favicon. Composite the supplied files only.
8. Never invent a colour, a font, a service, a price, an artist, a review, a credential or a history.
9. Anything marked `CONFIRM BEFORE PUBLISHING` waits on the studio. Build the slot, keep the marker visible, and do not fill it with a guess.

## Seven things to know before designing anything

1. **The logo is a raster and is composited, never drawn.** No vector exists. Use the supplied files in `assets/logos/` at or above their measured sizes: the full lockup at 128 px wide or more, the rose window for avatars of 64 px or more, the cross below that.
2. **Dark grounds only.** There is no light-background logo and gold on white fails contrast. No layout in this kit has a light page, the kit document included.
3. **The mark is gothic. The words are not.** No blackletter as live type, no second gothic face, no church vocabulary in the copy. Plain sentences in sentence case.
4. **Real photographs only.** Every tattoo, person and room is a file from `assets/photos/`. No stock. No AI-generated tattoos, skin, people, interiors or logos. No retouching of a tattoo.
5. **Every piece of work is credited to the artist who made it, by name.** All work on file is by Roger Cazares. Work by the other artists, and any photograph of the room, is a labelled photo slot.
6. **No price, and no claim about the business.** No figure, no minimum, no deposit, no discount. No ownership, no years, no licence or hygiene claim, no superlative, no premium adjective. The full register is at the end of file 6.
7. **Walk in, or book ahead.** Never "by appointment". Walk-ins are stated as a fact, never as a headline or a promotion.
