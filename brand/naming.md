**Read:** for a single-location Louisiana service business, out-of-state shops sharing
the name are **not a blocker.** Nobody in Livingston Parish is choosing between
Cathedral in Denham Springs and a closed shop in Salt Lake City. Three consequences,
though, and they are real:

1. **⚠️ A federal trademark on "Cathedral Tattoo" would likely be contested.** The term
   is in use by multiple shops. Pairs with **R-27** — if the logo is also
   AI-generated and unregistrable, the studio may have very little exclusive to
   defend. **Not legal advice; worth a lawyer's fifteen minutes before spending on
   signage.**
2. **SEO: never compete for the bare term "Cathedral Tattoo."** Own the geo-modified
   ones instead — "Cathedral Tattoo Studio Denham Springs", "tattoo shop Denham
   Springs", "black and grey tattoo Baton Rouge". This is already the right strategy
   for a local business and the prior art makes it mandatory.
3. **The handle needs to be unmistakably Cathedral's**, not the generic form, since the
   generic form is taken on Instagram anyway.

## Handle recommendations

Consistency across platforms matters more than elegance. In priority order:

1. `cathedraltattoostudio` — **if R-28 shows the studio owns it.** Matches the domain exactly.
2. `cathedraltattoola` — clear, geo-anchored, and unambiguous against the out-of-state shops.
3. `cathedraltattoo.ds` or `cathedraltattoo_ds` — only if 1 and 2 fail.

⚠️ **Do not rename or repoint `@rc_tattoos`.** It is Roger's personal artist brand and,
per `brand/architecture.md`, artist marks keep their own channels. It is also the
business's largest audience.

## Method and reliability

- **Domains:** `whois`, matching on no-match/not-found. Reliable.
- **Instagram:** fetched each profile and read the `<title>` and `og:description`. A
  control handle known not to exist returns a bare "Instagram" title, so the method
  distinguishes real accounts from missing ones. Reliable.
- **⚠️ TikTok: could not be determined.** Every handle tested, including a nonsense
  control, returned the same generic title — TikTok serves an app shell to
  non-browsers. **TikTok availability is unverified and must be checked by hand in a
  browser.** Recorded as unknown rather than guessed.
- Prior art: web search. Not a trademark search.
