# Photo manifest

Cathedral Tattoo Studio. Version 0.1, 2026-10-12. Prepared by BlakSheep Creative. Nothing here has been approved by anyone at the studio.

## What does not exist

Read this before designing any layout. None of the following is in this folder, and none may be drawn, generated, borrowed or implied.

- **No work by Mikayla Paul.** Every photograph here is Roger Cazares's work. An artist layout for anyone else is a labelled photo slot.
- **No interior or exterior of the studio.** No photograph of the room, the chair, the front desk, the door or the building at 1318 Florida Ave SW.
- **No at-work portrait that can be published.** The one portrait on file was rejected: see "Deliberately left out".
- **No healed-versus-fresh pairs.** No photograph is identified as healed or as fresh, so no caption says either word.
- **No process shots.** No stencil, no machine in hand, no drawing on paper. Rows 28 and 29 are finished designs on a tablet screen, which is the nearest thing on file.
- **No consented client faces.** Nobody who wears a tattoo in these photographs has been asked. No wearer's face is shown.
- **No piece identified as a cover-up.** Nothing on file is recorded as covering older work, so no caption says "cover-up" and no layout shows a before and after.
- **No landscape photographs.** All 29 files are portrait. The widest is 1714 px.
- **No record of where or when a piece was tattooed.** Captions name the piece, the placement and the artist. They never name a place or a date.

## Rules for the whole set

1. **Real photographs only.** These 29 files are the whole library. No stock, no generated image, no photograph from anywhere else.
2. **Caption form.** "[piece], [placement]. Roger Cazares." Sentence case. For rows 28 and 29: "[subject], original design. Roger Cazares." Use the captions in the table.
3. **Crop, straighten and correct exposure only.** No filter, no colour grade, no sharpening halo, no background blur, no background swap, no object removal, no generative fill, no expansion, no upscaling. Black and grey stays neutral: never warm it, never crush the blacks.
4. **Never retouch a tattoo.** Not the line, not the shading, not the redness around fresh work, not the skin.
5. **The RC monogram stays.** It is Roger Cazares's signature on his own photograph. Never erase it, cover it, recolour it or cut through it. A crop either keeps the whole monogram or leaves the whole monogram outside the frame. It is never treated as a logo, and it never sits beside the Cathedral lockup as an equal.
6. **Religious imagery is tattoo work, never a message.** Rows 4 to 9 show Christ, a crown of thorns, cherubs and a verse. They are shown as work with a plain caption. They are never a hero image, never a background, never beside a headline that could read as a statement of faith, and no words from a tattoo are lifted into a headline.
7. **Memorial and family pieces are shown plainly.** Rows 1, 2, 3 and 10. No caption says who the person or the animal was, no caption says "memorial" unless it is the piece name in the table, and nothing is said about loss.
8. **Backgrounds are not the studio.** No photograph shows a room that can be identified as 1318 Florida Ave SW. Never caption or crop a background as "the studio". Where the cautions say to crop a background, crop it.
9. **No wearer's face.** If a crop would bring a jaw, a mouth or a profile into view, crop tighter.
10. **No size above the file.** A file is never displayed wider than its pixel width, and never wider than half its pixel width where a 2x screen must be served sharply.
11. **Fewer, larger.** A page shows at most 12 of these, and a single view shows at most 4. See file 3, section 12.
12. **If anyone asks for a photograph to come down, it comes down the same day.**

## Deliberately left out

60 of the 89 candidates were rejected. Do not go looking for them.

- **The at-work portrait of Roger Cazares** (`05-Photos/roger-cazares-cathedral-tattoo-studio-denham-springs-owner-tattoo-artist-at-work-portrait.webp`, 800 × 1147). **A neon sign with another shop's name is on the wall behind him, top left, and the room is not Cathedral's.** It also carries the word "owner" in its file name, which the kit never uses. It may come back only as a crop that removes the whole back wall, checked by eye, and only after someone confirms the photograph may be used. `CONFIRM BEFORE PUBLISHING`. A second at-work frame from the same room was left out for the same reason, and at 800 px wide it is too small.
- **Cleaned copies where the patch changed the tattoo.** A floral chest and half sleeve, a reaper in a gothic window, a pilot skull half sleeve, a pair of melting eyes, a nine-tailed fox, three lightbulbs, a tall ship and a border collie portrait. Each is a strong piece. Each needs the unwatermarked original from the artist before it can be used.
- **Everything still watermarked.** The quarantine folder and the uncleaned originals were not opened for selection.
- **Licensed characters.** A film mask on a rib piece, and a dog design in the style of a cartoon series. Two more in the uncleaned set were never candidates.
