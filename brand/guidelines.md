## 1. The brand in one page

**Cathedral Tattoo Studio** — a tattoo studio at 1318 Florida Ave SW, Denham Springs,
serving the Denham Springs and Baton Rouge area.

- **Position:** premium commissioned work. Where a tattoo is treated as something with
  weight, not a purchase.
  🚨 **Under review (B-05).** The live shop takes walk-ins six days a week. Until
  Clint rules, build to the working line: *considered work, and a room that is easy
  to walk into.* Never write "by appointment" or "appointment only".
- **Hours:** Mon–Thu 10 AM–6 PM · Fri–Sat 10 AM–7 PM · Sun closed.
- **Artists:** Roger Cazares, Mikayla Paul, Cleo. Always named, never "our team".
  ⚠️ **Open tension:** there is no shop minimum. See `context.md` — premium currently
  has no price floor supporting it, and rests entirely on restraint and selectivity.
- **In one sentence:** *Cathedral is where people in Livingston Parish go when the
  tattoo has to mean something.*
- **Tagline (proposed):** **Work worth carrying.**
- **Differentiator:** congruence. A cathedral mark over a portfolio genuinely built on
  memorial, religious and fine-line work. The mark is a description, not a costume.
- **Front-facing brand:** **Cathedral.** Always.

**The two rules that govern everything else**
1. **The mark is gothic; the voice is not.** Let the logo be dramatic so the writing
   doesn't have to be.
2. **Premium is expressed, never claimed.** Restraint, selectivity, craft evidence and
   pace. Never the word.

## 2. Logo

**The mark ships as the existing raster** (decision, Clint 2026-09-05). Derived assets:
`01-Brand-Assets/derived/`.

### Minimum sizes — measured, not guessed

| Element | Floor | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Full lockup | **128px** | Site header, email, OG card, print |
| Rose window | **64px** | Social avatars |
| Cross | **16px** | Favicon, app icon, stamps, stickers |

Below 128px the full lockup is illegible (`06-Reports/logo-source-analysis.md`). **Do
not scale it down and hope.** Step to the next element instead.

### Clear space
Minimum clear space on all sides = **the height of the cross finial** at the top of the
mark, roughly 8% of the lockup's height. Nothing — type, image edge, button — enters it.

### Placement
- **Once per view.** The mark does not repeat down a page and does not follow the user
  in a sticky header at full size.
- Header: full lockup or horizontal crop, left or centred.
- Avatar: rose window (≥64px) or cross. **Never the full lockup squeezed into a circle.**
- Favicon: the cross. Always.

### Never
- ❌ Never place the mark on a light background. There is no light-background variant
  and gold on white fails contrast at 2.95:1.
- ❌ Never use the **RC monogram** as site chrome, favicon, avatar or logo slot. It is
  Roger's artist signature on his own work only (`architecture.md`).
- ❌ Never recolour, outline, add effects to, rotate, or stretch the mark.
- ❌ Never place it over busy imagery. If it must overlay a photo, use the alpha version
  on a dark, low-detail area — and only ever on dark.
- ❌ Never reconstruct or redraw parts of it to fill a space.

## 3. Colour

⚠️ Interim values, sampled from the raster. Authoritative source: `DESIGN.md` and
`website/css-tokens.css` — keep all three identical.

| Role | Token | Value | Contrast on ground |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ground | `--cts-bg-base` | `#0A0A0A` | — |
| Surface | `--cts-bg-surface` | `#141414` | — |
| Elevated | `--cts-bg-elevated` | `#1E1C19` | — |
| Gold primary | `--cts-primary` | `#B99040` | 6.72:1 ✅ AA |
| Gold bright | `--cts-gold-bright` | `#EBC853` | 12.18:1 ✅ AA |
| Gold deep | `--cts-gold-deep` | `#825F2C` | 3.42:1 ⚠️ large text only |
| Text | `--cts-text-primary` | `#E8E4DC` | 15.61:1 |
| Text muted | `--cts-text-muted` | `#A9A29A` | 7.85:1 |
| Border | `--cts-border-subtle` | `rgba(185,144,64,0.18)` | — |

**Rules**
- **Dark by default.** Black is the field, not the background — it is ~83% of the mark
  and should be roughly that share of any layout.
- **Gold is ~12–15% maximum.** A page that is half gold has misunderstood the brand,
  and under a premium position reads cheap rather than rich.
- ❌ **Never gold text on white** (2.95:1). If a light section is unavoidable, use
  `#825F2C` at large sizes only.
- ✅ Solid gold button, black label (`#0A0A0A` on `#B99040`) = 6.72:1. The correct CTA.
- No second accent hue. Status colours are UI only and never appear in brand material.

## 4. Typography

| Level | Face | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Logo / rare hero | Blackletter *(unidentified)* | Lockup only. Never below ~28px |
| H1 / H2 | **Cinzel** | Caps, letterspaced, short lines |
| H3 / subheads | **Source Sans 3** Semibold | Cinzel gets fussy this small |
| Body | **Source Sans 3** | 1.125rem, line-height 1.6 |
| UI / labels | Source Sans 3 | Sentence case |

Cinzel is drawn from Roman inscriptional capitals — the lettering carved into stone
buildings. It carries the architectural weight **without imitating the blackletter**,
which is the trap: a second gothic face turns the brand into costume.

❌ Avoid: any second blackletter, tattoo-script faces, distressed/grunge faces,
geometric sans (Futura, Poppins), slab serifs, handwritten faces.

⚠️ Recommendations, unconfirmed. The blackletter in the wordmark has never been
identified.

## 5. Voice

> **Cathedral speaks with plain warmth — specific, careful, and unhurried.**

Eight qualities, with do/don't examples, in `voice.md`: **Plain · Specific · Careful ·
Honest about limits · Unhurried · Selective · At ease · Attributive.**

**Review responses** (`voice.md` §09): thanks and first name, one specific thing they
said, the artist's name, two or three sentences, no exclamation marks. The responses
already live on the GBP are the reference.

**Style rules**
- Sentence case everywhere, headlines included.
- **No em dashes** in client-facing copy (BSC house rule + AI-detection signal).
- No exclamation marks. No ellipses.
- Contractions always. Active voice. Second person, singular.
- Paragraphs max four lines.
- Numerals for prices, hours, dates, sessions, sizes.

**Never say**
- Premium adjectives: premium, luxury, high-end, elite, exclusive, bespoke, boutique.
- Superlatives: premier, utopia, best, world-class. *(The first two are Tattootopia's.)*
- Category dead claims: "clean and sterile", "experienced", "all styles".
- Discount, promo or urgency language of any kind.
- "Ink" as a noun. "Parlor". "Bring your vision to life". "Journey".
- Any comparison to another local shop, named or unnamed.

## 6. Imagery

- **Dark, quiet, documentary.** Natural colour — no filters that muddy grey wash.
- **Show less, larger.** Twenty pieces at full width, not 97 thumbnails. A wall of
  thumbnails reads as a volume shop.
- **Sort by what the piece is** — portrait, memorial, fine line, cover-up, lettering —
  not by style jargon. That is what people search.
- **Show process.** Nobody local does; it costs nothing but a camera.
- Roger's RC monogram stays on his work. It is a signature, not a defect.
- ❌ No stock photography. No neon studio lighting. No glove-and-machine flat-lays.
- ⚠️ 8 prior-shop images still carry a competitor watermark — never publish
  (`WATERMARK-REMOVAL.md`). Two `99-Reference/` folders are never published at all.
