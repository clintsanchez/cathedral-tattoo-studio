## Body-art licensing — cannot be verified online

Louisiana regulates tattooing through the **LDH Office of Public Health, Food &
Drug Unit**, under the Sanitary Code Title 51 Part XXVIII. Both the **shop** (a
facility permit) and each **operator** (Commercial Body Art Operator
registration, Form FD-56) must be registered, with bloodborne-pathogen training
on file. There is **no public searchable registry** — LDH publishes application
forms only.

**Therefore:** get the shop permit number and each artist's operator
registration number from Roger in writing. Until then, publish **no** licensing,
sterilization, or compliance claim of any kind.
