# Brand Audit — Cathedral Tattoo Studio

*2026-09-05. **Baseline audit.***

> **Scored on what is live, not what is filed** — per the skill's own rule. The live
> brand is the Google Business Profile and the Facebook page, and nothing else: there
> is no website, no ads, no email, no Instagram on file. The brand package in
> `brand/` is documented *intent* and is used here only to measure intent against
> reality. **The gap between the two is the finding, not a failure of the package.**
>
> Re-run this after the site launches to measure movement.

**Read this score correctly.** 12/30 is not an indictment. It is what a four-month-old
business with no website looks like when measured against a full brand framework. The
mark is genuinely good and the work is genuinely strong; almost every point lost is
lost to *absence* rather than to error, and absence is cheap to fix. The exception is
Dimension 1, where there is a real strategic problem, not just a missing asset.

---

### Dimension 1 — Positioning clarity: 1/5 🔴

**In one sentence, what does the live brand stand for?** It cannot be answered. The
live presence states a category, a phone number and one day of opening hours ①.

**Could the positioning apply to a competitor?** Entirely. There is nothing to
distinguish. Every observable element — category, dark aesthetic, portfolio grid — is
shared with five local shops.

**What would be lost if it disappeared?** Today, from the market's point of view,
nothing that is publicly legible. Roger's clients would lose their artist, which is
the real equity, and it is currently invisible at the brand level.

The gothic mark implies a point of view that no published word supports. `context.md`
records the differentiator as `_TBD_` and forbids inventing one; `positioning.md`
proposes reverence and commissioned work, but **it is unapproved and unpublished**.
This is the only dimension where the deficit is strategic rather than logistical.

### Dimension 2 — Visual identity: 3/5 🟡

**The strongest dimension, and the mark is doing the work single-handed.**

- **Distinctive?** Yes, markedly. Gold-on-black blackletter over a cathedral facade is
  unlike anything else in the parish, where the category runs black/white/red
  (`competitors.md`).
- **Does it match the positioning?** Yes. This is the congruence the strategy rests on.
- **Three or more logo versions in active use?** ⚠️ **Yes, and this is the deduction.**
  The Cathedral lockup, the CTS badge, the RC monogram and a legacy RC-Tattoos skull
  sketch all circulate ①, and RC appears on nearly every portfolio image. To an
  outsider it is not obvious which brand they are looking at.
  `architecture.md` now resolves this; the resolution is not yet applied anywhere live.
- **⚠️ The mark exists only as raster.** No vector, so no favicon, no print, no
  signage, no clean reproduction at any size. A brand whose only asset is a Facebook
  screenshot cannot be applied consistently even in principle.
- **Typography and colour:** no system in use. The blackletter face is unidentified;
  the palette is sampled off a JPEG and appears nowhere but in `DESIGN.md`.

### Dimension 3 — Messaging consistency: 2/5 🔴

**Homepage hero:** none — no website. **Social bio vs. GBP:** both state the category
and little else, and they disagree on it — GBP says "Tattoo shop", Facebook says
"Tattoo & Piercing Shop" ①, and whether piercing is live is unknown. **Pitch:** none.

There is no value proposition to encounter in five seconds, and no proof points
anywhere. The one genuinely effective message the brand produces is Roger's monthly
availability post ② — which is on-strategy, converts, and is invisible to anyone not
already following the page.

### Dimension 4 — Voice & tone: 2/5 🔴

**Could you identify the brand from its copy with the logo removed?** No. The
published text is functional announcement copy.

**Three words for how it currently writes:** plain, sparse, functional.

The score is low for volume, not for quality. What exists is *right in register* —
`voice.md` is built on the plainness of Roger's own posts ② precisely because it is
the correct instinct. There is simply almost nothing published, no consistency to
measure across channels, and no distinctiveness yet. This is the cheapest dimension to
move.

### Dimension 5 — Audience alignment: 2/5 🔴

The brand is **not positioned where its audience is looking.** The audience research
says people find an artist through Instagram and TikTok, then validate on Google
(`audience.md`). Cathedral has **no Instagram or TikTok on file** and is effectively
absent from Google: no website, zero reviews, one published day of hours ①.

It does use the audience's language in the one place it speaks — "now booking",
"available", open dates ② — and the portfolio genuinely matches what the inferred
audience wants. But no audience definition exists on the client's side, and the
brand's only reachable channel is a 217-follower Facebook page ①.

⚠️ The audience work itself is entirely inferred. Zero reviews means zero customer
language. This dimension cannot exceed 3 until real customer input exists.

### Dimension 6 — Competitive differentiation: 2/5 🔴

**Immediately distinct from the top three competitors?** *Visually*, yes — the most
distinct in the parish. *Substantively*, no: a visitor comparing Cathedral with
Tattootopia, Faithful and Burning Lotus sees one shop with a working site and two with
strong review counts, against one with a nice logo, no site and no reviews.

**Clear reason to choose Cathedral over the alternatives?** Not yet published. The
reason exists — the portfolio — and is reachable only by scrolling a Facebook page.

Not "me too" in identity, but currently un-evidenced, which in practice loses to a
generic competitor holding 168 reviews ③.

## What's working

1. **The mark is the best in the local market.** Distinctive, category-appropriate,
   and congruent with the actual portfolio — an alignment none of the five competitors
   has. It is a real asset and it is under-exploited.
2. **The portfolio is deep and specific.** 97 filed pieces across memorial portraits,
   religious black and grey, fine line, dotwork, lettering and cover-ups ①. It carries
   almost all of the brand's current persuasive weight.
3. **The availability post is genuinely good marketing, invented by the client.**
   Publishing open dates month by month ② is honest scarcity, matches the
   appointment-led model, and no local competitor does it.
4. **The verbal instinct is right.** Roger's plain, direct posting register ② is
   exactly correct against a category of superlatives — worth protecting rather than
   "upgrading".
5. **The asset library is clean and filed.** 187 images with SEO filenames, IP and
   personal photos quarantined, watermark issues documented per file.

## What's not working

**1. No vector logo. 🔴 Highest priority.**
*Evidence:* the largest asset is a 1242px raster; the palette in `DESIGN.md` is
sampled from a JPEG.
*Impact:* blocks the favicon, OG card, print, signage, merchandise and the real colour
tokens. Blocks `brand-guidelines` entirely (`CLAUDE.md`). Every other identity task
queues behind this one.

**2. ~~Three unresolved addresses.~~ ✅ Resolved 2026-09-05. 🟢**
*Evidence:* GBP/FB showed Denham Springs, the SoS filing shows Baton Rouge, and an
August post announces an unnamed new location ①②. **Clint confirmed 1318 Florida Ave
SW is the operating studio.**
*Impact:* location page, LocalBusiness schema, citations, GBP, print and signage are
all unblocked. ⚠️ Residual: the move is still coming with no date, so build the NAP to
update in one place and hold citation volume proportionate until it resolves.

**3. Zero reviews against a competitor with ~168. 🔴**
*Evidence:* GBP and Facebook both show none ①; Burning Lotus shows 4.7 across ~168 ③.
*Impact:* the single largest competitive gap, and the one thing positioning cannot fix.
It is also the proof the entire "reverence" position depends on.

**4. Invisible in search. 🔴**
*Evidence:* no website, GBP still prompting "Add website", one published day of hours ①.
*Impact:* the audience's validation step happens on Google, and there is nothing to
find. ⚠️ Competitors' web presence is broken or absent, so this is the cheapest
advantage available — and it is being left on the table.

**5. Four competing marks in circulation. 🟡**
*Evidence:* Cathedral lockup, CTS badge, RC monogram, legacy RC skull ①, with RC on
nearly every portfolio image.
*Impact:* dilutes a brand that is only four months old. `architecture.md` resolves it
on paper; nothing is applied live.

**6. Unreachable on the audience's primary channels. 🟡**
*Evidence:* no Instagram or TikTok handle on file.
*Impact:* the fine-line persona is unreachable, and tattoo discovery is
Instagram-first.

## Priority recommendations

**Immediate (this week — no client dependency)**
- ✅ **Address confirmed. Start the GBP and local work** — this was issue 2 and it is
  now the fastest-moving item in the package.
- Send the client email. Three of the remaining five issues clear on Roger's answers.
  ⚠️ Add the vision/mission/values questions and the origin interview first
  (`strategy.md`, `story.md`) — the current draft is missing both.
- Resolve the Tattootopia conflict internally. BSC now advises two shops on the same
  street; it gates the story and any competitive copy.
- Apply `architecture.md` to what already exists: shop avatar to the Cathedral or CTS
  mark, never RC.

**Short-term (30–60 days — unblocked by the client's answers)**
- Vector recovery, then resample the palette and update `DESIGN.md` and
  `css-tokens.css` together.
- Build the site. Dark, portfolio-led, sorted by what the piece *is*, artists named,
  availability as the conversion surface. ✅ Location page and LocalBusiness schema
  unblocked on 1318 Florida Ave SW.
- Complete the GBP: real hours, services, photos, and the website link once it exists.
- **Start review generation the week the address settles.** Highest-leverage
  non-brand action available.
- Claim the Instagram and TikTok handles; claim `cathedraltattoostudio.com`.

**Long-term (strategic)**
- Get the positioning approved and *lived* — reverence proven through process
  documentation, healed-work posts and consult quality, not asserted in copy.
- Run `brand-guidelines` once the vector lands, to lock the system before the roster
  grows.
- Set the artist-attribution rule at the point of each hire.
- Re-run this audit after launch. **Target: 22/30 within six months of a live site,
  a confirmed address, and the first twenty reviews.**
