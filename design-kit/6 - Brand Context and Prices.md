## 6. The pricing rule

There are no prices and there is no minimum. Section 6 is a rule, not a list.

- **No price is published.** No figure, no "from" price, no range, no hourly rate, no day rate, no shop minimum, no total. A tattoo is quoted per piece, by a person, in conversation.
- **No minimum is stated in either direction.** The studio has no shop minimum (stated by Clint Sanchez, 2026-09-05). That fact is never published: not "no minimum", not "no job too small", not "small tattoos welcome".
- **No deposit wording.** No "deposit", "holds your date", "non-refundable" or "comes off the price". Whether deposits are taken is not on file.
- **Nothing about affordability, in either direction.** No "affordable", "price friendly", "fair", "reasonable", "competitive", "cheap", "worth it", "worth every penny", "investment" or "you get what you pay for".
- **No offer language.** No discount, promo, sale, deal, special, giveaway, raffle, countdown or "spots going fast".
- **No price comparison**, with any shop or any other kind of purchase.
- **Cost questions are answered with what the artist needs to know:** "Tell us what you want tattooed, roughly where, and roughly what size, and we'll tell you what it'll take. Call or text (225) 301-9527."
- **Price slots stay empty.** A template has no price field. If a reference layout has one, replace it with the "Call or text (225) 301-9527" button.
- **A customer praised the price in a review.** That is theirs to say. No sentence from a review that mentions price or what was paid is used as a quote.
- Payment methods, financing, cancellation and refunds: `CONFIRM BEFORE PUBLISHING`. None is on file.

### 9.1 The field, by territory

Denham Springs is a crowded market. Three tattoo shops, Cathedral among them, sit on the same street inside one mile, and there are a dozen options before a customer leaves the area. Almost every shop makes the same three claims in the same order (clean, experienced, custom) over a dark page with white type and a grid of tattoo photographs.

| Territory | What the shop in it does | What that means for Cathedral, stated about Cathedral only |
|---|---|---|
| **Established authority** | Leads with tenure, awards and superlatives. Has the most complete website in the field | Cathedral makes no tenure, award or superlative claim. The words "premier" and "utopia" in particular belong to this territory and are prohibited |
| **Faith and family** | Leads with family, faith and community, in a warm domestic register. Real review volume | Cathedral has no denominational voice. Religious imagery appears only as tattoo work in photographs |
| **The trusted default** | Leads with range ("all styles") and has by far the most reviews in the field, about 168 at 4.7 on 2026-09-05 | Cathedral does not compete on review count and never says "all styles". Its rating line is small, exact and dated |
| **Convenience** | Leads with walk-ins and six-day hours | Cathedral now has similar hours and takes walk-ins. It states both as facts and builds no headline on them. It differs on the work and the mark |
| **The long tail** | Shops with little brand presence, mostly on Facebook only | No bearing on design |
