## Where this client departs from the universal prompt

- No light templates and no light variant of anything. The logo has no light-background version.
- The logo is a raster with size floors, so feed cards that show work carry no logo.
- Two Instagram accounts exist. The system covers the studio account only; the artist's own account is out of scope by rule.
- Families added for the trade: finished piece with artist credit, healed work, process, open dates calendar, walk-in notice, first time, portrait reference, cover-up answers, aftercare, meet the artist.
- Families dropped: price and promotions, piercing, seasonal sales, before and after on memorial work, charts, comparison, hiring, the move, events, memes, commentary.
- Photo file names are not listed in the prompt. `assets/photos/PHOTO-MANIFEST.md` is the list of record.

## Decisions made in file 7 that the shared brief did not state

- The lockup is placed at 400 px or wider on a 1080 px canvas, so it shows at the 128 px floor in a feed.
- The hashtag list, the limit of five, and the ban on `#MemorialTattoo`.
- The walk-in notice uses no Cinzel, so walk-ins are never a display headline.
- The review card carries a non-printing `checked` date instead of a printed review date.
- The rating line carries no star at all.
- "See the work" points to the Instagram profile or the Google photographs until a website exists.

**Success.** The system reads as one design system producing many formats, and every graphic unmistakably belongs to this studio: a black field, one line of gold, one real tattoo given all the room, the name of the artist who made it, and a plain sentence that is true. Load another client's tokens and assets and the architecture still works, with nothing of this client left behind.
